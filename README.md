A 100-word villain monologue for make.fun's "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue" bounty, 2 dollars in USDC. A fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — the tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm and the deflections, never in a plain statement — with a scheme and a history behind him.

> Come in, hero. Mind the— no, the trapdoor is fine, I had it oiled. Tuesdays. The minions unionised, did you know? They want breaks. Breaks! From evil. I spent three centuries building the Obsidian Spire, and nobody warns you about the paperwork. Curses in triplicate. The dragon left — said the commute was killing him. And now you, sword raised, monologuing at me like I'm not already running on four hours of sleep and one cold cup of— ...Look. Take the amulet, hero, please. Honestly. I've been trying to lose it since the solstice, and the prophecy never mentioned the admin.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`.
